# Complaint procedure - non-conformity of goods with the contract

Checked: 2026-11-06. Scope: consumer non-conformity claims for goods, including consumer in-store complaints on supported non-conformity points (distance-return rules do not apply in store).

Source baseline: S1 distance-sales terms effective 2026-03-05; earlier orders or uncertain applicability require review of the applicable historical terms (S1 preamble, I.1). For in-store consumer complaints, the supported non-conformity context comes from S3; do not claim that distance-sales company terms govern the in-store contract.

Sources: S1 https://www.x-kom.pl/regulamin (sections IX.58-60) ; S3 https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/reklamacja/niezgodnosc/ (section Niezgodność towaru z umową). UOKiK pages expose no revision date; only the checked date is recorded.

## Filing (S1 IX.58-60)
- A non-conformity claim may be made in any form; the official complaint form is recommended (https://www.x-kom.pl/reklamacje). This prototype never submits anything.

## Rights and remedies (S3)
- The seller is responsible for non-conformity appearing within two years of delivery. Repair/replacement and conditional price reduction/withdrawal are described by S3. Do not decide that remedy conditions are met or impose a sequence without the supported facts.
- Elapsed baseline time alone does not establish expired rights.

## Evidence limits
- A single photo cannot diagnose invisible faults, cause, authenticity, function, hidden surfaces or legal entitlement. Absence of visible damage does not mean absence of a fault.
- No automatic refund or claim rejection is made; no staff handoff occurs.

## Review triggers
- Business purchase, in-store return request, unknown channel, unsupported or old-source facts, conflicting facts: review. Review status, once set, is permanent.
