# Return procedure - withdrawal from a distance contract

Checked: 2026-11-06. Scope: qualifying private distance purchases from x-kom online orders under the distance-sales terms effective 2026-03-05 (orders placed up to 2026-03-04 follow historical terms and require review).

Sources: S1 https://www.x-kom.pl/regulamin ; S2 https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/prawo-odstapienia-od-umowy/terminy-odstapienie/ (section Terminy) ; S4 https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/prawo-odstapienia-od-umowy/koszty/ (section Koszty). UOKiK pages expose no revision date; only the checked date is recorded.

## Who may withdraw
- S1 I.1: a private purchase can include a natural person's non-professional business purchase. This prototype never decides that legal status. For business buyers, advise review and ask only entity type and professional purpose, never identifying data.

## Notification (S1 VIII.49-51; S2 Terminy)
- Qualifying private distance purchase: 15 days after receipt to notify withdrawal, no reason needed (S1 VIII.49-51). This is x-kom's commercial period, not the statutory minimum; S2 Terminy states 14 days counted from the day after receipt.
- Official return form is recommended (https://www.x-kom.pl/zwroty); notification by email or post is also allowed (S1 VIII.50-51). This prototype never submits anything.
- Unusual delivery arrangements, deadline extensions and uncertain boundary cases require review. Do not state a definitive legal deadline.

## Exceptions (S1 VIII.52)
- Personalized products, specified opened sealed goods, and inseparable combinations. Where exception facts are uncertain, require review.

## Condition and value (S1 VIII.53; S4 Koszty)
- Diminished value is distinct from loss of the withdrawal right; visible damage alone cannot reject a return (S1 VIII.53).
- Handling as in a shop is allowed for inspection; excessive handling causing diminished value may affect the refund (S4). No automatic photo-based denial or refund calculation.

## Return dispatch (S1 VIII.55)
- Dispatch within 14 days after notification, separate from the notification period.

## Review triggers
- Business or in-store purchase, unknown channel, order date before 2026-03-05 or uncertain applicability, unsupported or old-source facts, conflicting facts: review. Review status, once set, is permanent.
